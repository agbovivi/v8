Biography

Andrew F. Beathen is the founding and managing partner of Meston Law LLP. Over a career spanning more than three decades, he has established himself as one of the foremost advisers on complex cross-border corporate transactions, with particular depth in multi-jurisdictional mergers, acquisitions, and restructurings involving the United Kingdom, European Union, United States, and emerging markets in Asia and the Middle East.

Andrew founded the firm in 1989 with a clear vision: to create a practice that combined the intimacy and partner-level attention of a boutique with the geographic coverage and regulatory fluency required by multinational enterprises. Under his leadership, Meston Law has grown from a four-lawyer London practice to a firm of more than 85 attorneys across fourteen jurisdictions, while maintaining the culture of uncompromising standards that defined its earliest matters.

His practice focuses on transactions where complexity is not merely scale, but the intersection of multiple regulatory regimes, conflicting stakeholder interests, and compressed timelines. He has advised on some of the most consequential cross-border deals of the past two decades, including the restructuring of a pan-European energy conglomerate across six jurisdictions, the acquisition of a Asian-Pacific logistics platform by a Middle Eastern sovereign wealth fund, and the defence of a FTSE 100 company against a hostile bid involving simultaneous regulatory filings in London, Brussels, and Washington.

Andrew is admitted to practice in England and Wales, New York, Hong Kong, and Singapore, and holds rights of audience before the English Commercial Court and the Court of Appeal. He is a Fellow of the Institute of Directors and has served as a trustee of the London Legal Support Trust since 2015.

He read law at the University of Cambridge (St John's College) and completed the BCL at Magdalen College, Oxford. He began his career at Slaughter and May before founding Meston Law at the age of 29.

Representative Matters

A selection of transactions and matters that illustrate the depth and breadth of Andrew's practice.

2025 | Cross-Border M&A

Pan-Asian Logistics Platform Acquisition

Advised a Middle Eastern sovereign wealth fund on the £4.2 billion acquisition of a pan-Asian logistics and supply chain platform, spanning regulatory approvals in Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan, and the United Kingdom. Structured the transaction to achieve both antitrust clearance and foreign investment screening compliance within a compressed twelve-week timeline.

M&A Regulatory Cross-Border
2024 | Restructuring

European Energy Conglomerate Restructuring

Led the legal restructuring of a €6.8 billion European energy conglomerate across six jurisdictions (UK, Germany, France, Netherlands, Spain, Italy), involving the separation of generation, transmission, and retail assets, the negotiation of inter-creditor arrangements with twelve lending institutions, and the simultaneous filing of scheme of arrangement proceedings in London and Frankfurt.

Restructuring Finance Multi-Jurisdictional
2023 | Defence M&A

FTSE 100 Hostile Bid Defence

Advised the board of a FTSE 100 pharmaceutical company in defending a £18 billion hostile takeover bid, coordinating defence strategy across London, Brussels, and Washington regulatory counsel. Successfully secured CMA Phase 1 clearance and EC merger control approval, contributing to the bidder's withdrawal after eight months of contested proceedings.

Defence Antitrust Strategic
2022 | Private Equity

Sponsor-Led Cross-Border Carve-Out

Represented a leading European private equity sponsor in the €2.1 billion carve-out and acquisition of a global industrial components division from a US-listed parent, involving transitional services agreements across seventeen countries, employee transfer mechanisms under TUPE and analogous regimes, and the simultaneous closing of a €1.4 billion senior secured facilities agreement.
