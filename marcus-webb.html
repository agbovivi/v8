Biography

Marcus Webb is a Partner in Morag Law Group LLP's Banking & Finance practice and co-head of the firm's New York office. With a quarter-century of experience, he has established a distinguished track record in complex project finance, syndicated lending, and structured products.

Marcus's practice centers on advising leading international financial institutions, multinational corporations, and project sponsors on energy infrastructure, telecommunications, and transportation financing across North America, Europe, and Asia. He has arranged and structured over $15 billion in project and corporate finance, with particular expertise in export credit financing, blended finance structures, and regulatory capital treatment of structured products.

He joined Morag Law Group in 2015 as co-leader of the New York office, bringing decades of market leadership from leading international finance practices. His appointment reflected the firm's commitment to expanding its banking and finance capabilities across the Americas.

Marcus is admitted to practice in New York, Pennsylvania, and England and Wales. He serves on the advisory boards of several finance industry associations and is a frequent speaker at major financial and legal conferences worldwide. He is a member of the American Bar Association and International Bar Association.

He holds a BA in Economics from Yale University and a JD from Columbia University School of Law, where he was an editor of the Columbia Business Law Review. He is fluent in English and French.
