Where Independent Counsel
Meets Global Reach

Founded in 1989, Morag Law Group LLP has built its reputation on principled advocacy: complex legal challenges spanning continents demand both deep expertise and true independence. Our partners maintain full decision-making authority and align exclusively with clients whose values and ambitions reflect our own commitment to excellence.

Our attorneys are admitted to practice across fourteen jurisdictions, with deep fluency in common law, civil law, and Islamic legal traditions. We anticipate legal evolution, not merely react to it—positioning clients strategically years before regulatory or market shifts materialize.
